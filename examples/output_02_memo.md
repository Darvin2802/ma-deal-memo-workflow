# Example Output 02 — XCF Global / Focus Impact BH3

This is a public example output based on a real transaction clue and public-source research. It is included to demonstrate the structure and style of the workflow.

## 1. Transaction Identification

**Input headline:** XCF Global mergers with Focus Impact BH3; 11 Mar 2024

**Verified transaction title:** Business combination between Focus Impact BH3 Acquisition Company and XCF Global Capital, Inc. under a March 11, 2024 Business Combination Agreement.

**Transaction type:** Merger / de-SPAC business combination. Using the controlled vocabulary, the closest fit is merger. The primary documents show a two-step structure in which BHAC merged into Merger Sub 1 and Merger Sub 2 merged into XCF, leaving XCF as a wholly owned subsidiary of NewCo, which became the public company.

**Verification summary:**  
The March 12, 2024 announcement and BHAC’s March 12, 2024 8-K confirm the signed transaction. The June 12, 2025 XCF Global 8-K and BHAC’s June 30, 2025 deregistration filing confirm consummation effective June 6, 2025.

**Confidence in transaction identification:** High. The counterparties, agreement date, structure, later amendments, waiver, stockholder approval, and closing are all confirmed in SEC and company-source materials.

## 2. Transaction Snapshot

**Deal headline used for research:** XCF Global mergers with Focus Impact BH3

**Verified transaction title:** Business combination between Focus Impact BH3 Acquisition Company and XCF Global Capital, Inc.

**Announcement date:** March 12, 2024. The press release was issued March 12, 2024 and refers to a Business Combination Agreement dated March 11, 2024.

**Signing date:** March 11, 2024.

**Closing date:** June 6, 2025.

**Current status:** Closed.

**Buyer(s):** Focus Impact BH3 Acquisition Company, Focus Impact BH3 NewCo, Inc., Focus Impact BH3 Merger Sub 1, LLC, and Focus Impact BH3 Merger Sub 2, Inc. were the acquisition-side entities in the signed structure.

**Seller(s):** No separate cash seller was identified in the primary business-combination documents. Instead, XCF equityholders exchanged their XCF equity for NewCo stock.

**Target / asset / company:** XCF Global Capital, Inc.

**Stake acquired / sold:** 100% of XCF common stock outstanding immediately before the company merger was converted into NewCo common stock; XCF survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of NewCo.

**Geography:** United States; BHAC was a Delaware SPAC and XCF was a Nevada corporation. XCF’s announced operating footprint centered on Reno, Nevada, with additional sites in North Carolina and Florida.

**Deal value:** XCF was valued in the agreement at a $1.75 billion pre-money equity value, subject to net debt and transaction-expense adjustments, and the announcement stated an implied pro forma enterprise value of $1.84 billion for the combined company.

**Consideration mix:** Stock consideration in NewCo / New XCF shares. No primary source was retrieved confirming standalone cash consideration to XCF holders at signing.

## 7. Transaction Details

**Announcement date:** March 12, 2024  
**Signing date:** March 11, 2024  
**Closing date:** June 6, 2025  
**Current status:** closed

**Headline deal value / valuation fields:**
- **Pre-money equity value of XCF:** $1.75 billion
- **Implied pro forma enterprise value of combined company:** $1.84 billion
- **Cash consideration:** No confirmed cash consideration to XCF holders found in retrieved sources
- **Equity consideration:** XCF shares converted into NewCo / New XCF shares at $10.00 per share, subject to net debt and transaction-expense adjustments
- **Debt assumed / net debt adjustment:** The agreement summary confirms the XCF equity value was subject to adjustments for net debt and transaction expenses. Retrieved summary materials do not provide a clean standalone “debt assumed” headline figure for the business combination itself
- **Working capital adjustment:** Not separately confirmed in retrieved sources
- **Earnout / contingent consideration:** Not confirmed in retrieved sources for the business combination itself
- **Enterprise value / equity value / implied value:** as above

**% acquired / sold:** 100% of XCF common stock outstanding immediately before the company merger converted into NewCo stock, with XCF surviving as a wholly owned NewCo subsidiary

**Transaction structure:** Two-step merger

**Quoted clause:**  
“the business combination will be effected in two steps: (a) BHAC will merge with and into Merger Sub 1 … and (b) immediately following the NewCo Merger, Merger Sub 2 will merge with and into XCF.”

**Plain-English explanation:**  
First, the public SPAC was folded into a NewCo subsidiary. Second, XCF was merged into another NewCo subsidiary. The result was a new listed holding company above XCF.

**Consideration mechanics**

**Quoted clause:**  
each XCF share was converted into NewCo stock “based on a pre-money equity value of XCF of $1,750,000,000, subject to adjustments for net debt and transaction expenses, and a price of $10.00 per share.”

**Plain-English explanation:**  
XCF holders were paid in stock, not a fixed cash amount. The exchange ratio floated based on XCF’s net debt and deal expenses.

**Conditions precedent:**  
Customary approvals and conditions included BHAC stockholder approval, XCF stockholder approval, S-4 effectiveness, HSR waiting-period expiration or termination, exchange listing approval, and absence of legal restraints. BHAC also had conditions tied to the closing of the New Rise acquisitions and execution of an amended key supply/offtake agreement.

**Regulatory approvals:**  
HSR expiration/termination and SEC effectiveness of the registration statement were expressly required.

**Board approval:**  
The March 2024 press release states both XCF’s and Focus Impact BH3’s boards approved the proposed business combination.

**Shareholder approval:**  
Focus Impact stockholders approved the business combination at a special meeting held February 27, 2025.

**Financing / expense support details:**  
Focus Impact’s 2025 10-Q states XCF agreed to pay or reimburse all “Reimbursable Expenses,” including government filing fees, advisor fees, audit/accounting fees, excise taxes, and listing-related fees.

**Termination rights:**  
The agreement allowed termination by mutual consent, for failed approvals, uncured breaches, legal prohibition, specified key-agreement failure, or failure to close by the termination date. The original outside date was September 11, 2024, auto-extended to November 11, 2024 if the registration statement had not yet been declared effective. That outside date was later amended again through subsequent amendments.

**Reverse break fee:**  
No confirmed reverse break fee found in retrieved sources.

**Competing bids / go-shop:**  
No confirmed competing-bid process found in retrieved sources. The 8-K summary does state the parties agreed not to initiate negotiations or enter into agreements for certain alternative transactions.

**Post-signing amendments / waivers:**
- Amendment No. 1 extended the termination date from November 11, 2024 to March 31, 2025
- Amendment No. 2 was dated April 4, 2025
- Amendment No. 3 was dated May 30, 2025
- On June 5, 2025 the parties waived certain condition issues, including board-composition matters, certain New Rise issues, an unwaived Phillips 66 right of first refusal on the Reno facility, the absence of the amended key agreement, and the absence of company service-level insurance coverage

**Closing share issuance detail:**  
At closing, NewCo issued 142,120,364 shares to XCF equityholders and 389,359 shares to non-redeeming stockholders; New XCF had about 149.3 million shares outstanding after closing.

## 8. Asset / Company Details

### A. Confirmed from transaction documents

- XCF was described in the March 12, 2024 announcement as a company focused on sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) and other clean-burning biofuels
- At announcement, XCF stated it expected an initial annualized production capacity of 38 million gallons after the closing of its New Rise Renewables acquisition in Reno, Nevada
- XCF also stated it had acquired strategic locations in Wilson, North Carolina and Ft. Myers, Florida, and expected those sites to support future SAF expansion
- The Business Combination Agreement recitals tie the transaction to XCF’s expected acquisition of New Rise Renewables, LLC and New Rise SAF Renewables Limited Liability Company, including a renewable diesel production facility and an SAF facility in Reno, Nevada

### B. Supplemental from company / asset websites, filings, and other reliable sources

- XCF’s 2024 Form 10-K states that on December 8, 2023 it signed membership-interest purchase agreements to acquire New Rise entities. For New Rise Renewables, XCF agreed to issue a $100 million convertible promissory note plus 88,750,000 XCF common shares; for New Rise SAF, XCF agreed to issue 20,000,000 XCF common shares
- The same 10-K states the New Rise SAF acquisition closed on January 23, 2025 and the New Rise acquisition closed on February 19, 2025. It further states the New Rise Reno production facility is a renewable fuels processing plant in Reno, Nevada, and that the adjacent 10-acre plot was intended for “New Rise Reno 2” to produce SAF
- XCF’s June 6, 2025 closing press release states the New Rise Reno facility was operational and revenue-generating, had a nameplate production capacity of 38 million gallons of neat SAF per year, and that first customer deliveries of neat SAF were completed in March 2025
- XCF’s current corporate overview describes the company as one of the few publicly traded renewable-fuels companies primarily focused on SAF in the United States and states that New Rise Reno was in ramp-up stage. This is a later company-web supplemental source, not a transaction-date primary source

**Energy-transition relevance:**  
The business is centered on SAF production, aviation decarbonization, renewable-fuels infrastructure, and expansion of U.S. low-carbon fuel capacity. That framing is explicit in the March 2024 and June 2025 company materials.
