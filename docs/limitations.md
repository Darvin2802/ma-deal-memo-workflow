# Limitations

This workflow is designed to improve transaction research quality, but it has limits.

## Public disclosure limits

Some deals, especially private-company and private-asset transactions, may have limited public disclosure.

In those cases, the workflow may confirm the existence of a transaction but not all terms.

## Ownership visibility limits

Buyer and seller ownership chains are not always fully visible in public documents.

The workflow should stop where confirmation stops.

## Filing access limits

Some filings, agreements, or registry records may be:
- paywalled
- archived
- removed
- available only in local systems
- identified but not accessible

These should be labeled clearly.

## Language limits

Some relevant materials may be available only in local-language documents. Translation quality can affect precision.

## Advisor identification limits

Advisors are not always named in official materials. In those cases, identification may depend on secondary sources such as law firm matter pages or advisor deal announcements.

## Timing limits

Immediate post-announcement outputs may miss later updates such as:
- closing
- amendment
- termination
- competing bid
- revised consideration

The workflow should re-check status when the transaction remains relevant.

## Not a substitute for legal diligence

This workflow is a research and memo framework. It is not legal advice, investment advice, or formal diligence.
